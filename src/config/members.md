# FAQs

<details><summary>
When I become a member, how soon can I start booking sessions?
</summary>

Access requests typically take 3-7 working days, though due to the occasional clerical error, please allow up to 2 weeks to contact us before you wish to start booking sessions.

</details>

<details><summary>
Can students outside of the Engineering department use our spaces?
</summary>

Yes, but with restrictions. As a facility, we prioritise the activities of the academics who are directly involved in the facility's funding and the students who's research directly involve in the studio spaces.

</details>

<details><summary>
Can I borrow equipment loan without being inducted?
</summary>

Yes, but you will need to be an academic, or have your academic supervisor sponsor your request.
As with a typical equipment loan request, you will need to provide a supporting statement, and we reserve the right to decline your request.

</details>
