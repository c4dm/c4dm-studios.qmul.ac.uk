# C4DM Studios

C4DM Studios is a professional recording studio, performance space, and research facility based within the School of Electronic Engineering and Computer Science (EECS) at Queen Mary University of London.
C4DM Studios was originally established by the [Centre for Digital Music (C4DM)](https://c4dm.eecs.qmul.ac.uk/) and [Cognitive Science Research Group (CogSci)](https://cogsci.eecs.qmul.ac.uk/), and are now also working in collaboration with the [Centre for Multimodal AI (CMAI)](https://www.seresearch.qmul.ac.uk/cmai/).
Our facilities support research and teaching activities across fields including audio engineering, digital signal processing, human-computer interaction, music informatics, music perception and cognition, augmented instrument design, immersive technologies, AI and music, and performance studies.

We also offer our facilities for hire to external clients, such as recording artists, sound engineers, composers, external researchers and manufacturers. We actively support music production, recording, mixing & mastering, band rehearsals, performance capture, small concerts, research presentations, seminars, as well as specialised research projects. Commercial bookings can be arranged by contacting us via email at <a href='mailto:c4dm-studios@qmul.ac.uk'>c4dm-studios@qmul.ac.uk</a>
