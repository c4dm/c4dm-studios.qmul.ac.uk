# C4DM Studios

C4DM Studios is a professional recording studio, performance space, and research facility based in Mile End. We operate from within the School of Electronic Engineering and Computer Science (EECS) at Queen Mary University of London.

Our facility primarily serves to support research and teaching activities relating to fields of audio engineering, digital signal processing, human-computer interaction, music informatics, music perception and cognition, augmented instruments, immersive technologies, AI & music, and performance studies.

We extend our operation to commercial partners such as recording artists, touring parties,

Established by the [Centre for Digital Music (C4DM)](https://c4dm.eecs.qmul.ac.uk/) and [Cognitive Science Research Group (CogSci)](https://cogsci.eecs.qmul.ac.uk/), C4DM Studios are now also working in collaboration with the [Centre for Multimodal AI (CMAI)](https://www.seresearch.qmul.ac.uk/cmai/).
We are currently a core facility supporting the Centres for Doctoral Training in [Media & Arts Technology (MAT)](https://mat.qmul.ac.uk/) and [AI & Music (AIM)](https://www.aim.qmul.ac.uk/), as well as the [MSc Sound & Music Computing](https://www.qmul.ac.uk/postgraduate/taught/coursefinder/courses/sound-and-music-computing-msc/) course.
